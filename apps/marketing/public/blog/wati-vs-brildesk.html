Wati vs. BrilDesk: Which WhatsApp Team Inbox Is Right for Your Sales Team?

Looking for a Wati alternative that is built for sales, not broadcasts? Here is an honest comparison to help you decide.

Wati is one of the most popular WhatsApp Business API platforms, and for good reason. It was one of the first tools to make WhatsApp team inboxes accessible to small and mid-sized businesses. But if you are running a sales team, not a marketing or support operation, Wati may not be the best fit.

This comparison breaks down where Wati excels, where it falls short for sales teams, and why BrilDesk is built as a Wati alternative specifically for revenue-focused teams.

Overview of Both Tools

Wati

Wati (WhatsApp Team Inbox) launched in 2020 as a WhatsApp Business API solution focused on broadcast messaging, chatbot automation, and customer support. It has grown to serve thousands of businesses, primarily in India and Southeast Asia.

Core strength: Broadcast campaigns and no-code chatbot builder. If you need to send promotional messages to large lists and automate support FAQs, Wati does this well.

BrilDesk

BrilDesk is a WhatsApp shared inbox built from the ground up for sales teams. Instead of starting with broadcasts and adding inbox features, BrilDesk starts with the sales workflow: lead assignment, pipeline management, team performance tracking, and conversation-to-deal conversion.

Core strength: Sales team collaboration on WhatsApp. Built for teams of 5-50 reps who need to manage leads, close deals, and track performance from a single WhatsApp number.

Who Is Each Tool Best For?

Choose Wati if you need:

  • Large-scale broadcast campaigns (10,000+ contacts)
  • Complex chatbot flows for customer support
  • Shopify and e-commerce integrations for order notifications
  • A mature platform with a large user community
  • Marketing automation on WhatsApp

Choose BrilDesk if you need:

  • A WhatsApp inbox designed for sales workflows
  • Lead assignment by geography, language, or skill
  • Built-in pipeline and deal tracking
  • Rep performance analytics and response time tracking
  • Follow-up reminders and stale lead alerts
  • CRM sync without the CRM price tag

5 Key Differences Explained

1. Broadcast-First vs. Sales-First

Wati was built around the broadcast use case. Its strongest features are campaign management, template approval workflows, and audience segmentation. BrilDesk was built around the sales inbox. Its strongest features are lead routing, deal tracking, and rep performance. If you are looking for a Wati alternative because broadcasts are not your priority, this is the fundamental difference.

2. Lead Assignment Logic

Wati offers basic round-robin assignment. BrilDesk lets you auto-assign leads based on geography (route UAE leads to your Dubai team), language (Hindi speakers go to your Mumbai reps), or custom rules (VIP customers go to senior reps). For sales teams with specialized reps, this matters.

3. Pipeline Visibility

Wati does not have a pipeline view. You see conversations, but you cannot see where a deal stands in your sales process. BrilDesk includes a Kanban-style pipeline where you can move contacts through stages like Qualified, Proposal Sent, Negotiation, and Won. This is table stakes for any sales tool and a big reason teams look for a Wati alternative.

4. Follow-Up Automation

Wati's automation is centered on chatbot flows (if a customer says X, reply with Y). BrilDesk's automation is centered on sales workflows: if a lead has not responded in 24 hours, remind the rep. If a deal has been stale for 3 days, escalate to the manager. These are the automations that prevent leads from dying in the inbox.

5. Analytics That Sales Managers Care About

Wati's analytics focus on message delivery, read rates, and chatbot performance. BrilDesk's analytics focus on what sales managers need: average response time per rep, conversation-to-deal conversion rate, deals in pipeline by rep, and revenue attribution. If your job is to manage a sales team, not a marketing campaign, BrilDesk gives you the dashboards that matter.

The Verdict

Wati is a solid product. If your primary use case is sending WhatsApp broadcast campaigns or building support chatbots, it is a good choice. We are not here to say otherwise.

But if you are a sales team that needs to manage leads, track deals, monitor rep performance, and close revenue through WhatsApp, Wati was not built for that. It is a marketing and support tool that happens to have a shared inbox.

BrilDesk is a sales tool that happens to run on WhatsApp. That is the difference, and it is the reason teams searching for a Wati alternative are making the switch.

If your team's WhatsApp conversations are the deal, not the notification, BrilDesk is built for you.
